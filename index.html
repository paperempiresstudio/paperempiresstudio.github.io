Paper Empires Analytics

An internal reporting tool of the YouTube channel Paper Empires.

Paper Empires is a YouTube channel with business explainers through the eyes of an investor, and the economics of AI.

Paper Empires Analytics is a small, private tool that reads the channel's own statistics from YouTube once a day (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate). The channel team uses these numbers to evaluate and improve its videos.

What it does and does not do

  • It only reads data of the Paper Empires channel, with read-only permissions (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly).
  • It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
  • It is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up and no other users.
