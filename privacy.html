Privacy Policy

Paper Empires Analytics · effective 11 November 2026

Who we are

Paper Empires Analytics is an internal tool of the YouTube channel Paper Empires (@PaperEmpiresDecoded). Contact: paperempires.studio1@gmail.com.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's authorisation through Google OAuth, the tool reads, through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs, only data about the Paper Empires channel itself: the list of its videos and aggregated statistics such as views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions, click-through rate, likes and subscribers gained. These statistics are aggregated by YouTube and do not identify individual viewers.

How the data is used

The data is used only to evaluate and improve the channel's own videos. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

Storage and security

The statistics are stored in a private repository that only the channel team can access. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for evaluating the channel and can be deleted at any time on request. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time through the Google Account security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data processed by Google is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
